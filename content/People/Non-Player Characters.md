Location: [[Daggerford Overview| Daggerford]]

## Azam (Black Road Trader) 

Location: [[Black Road]]

Azam has been travelling the Black Road for decades and knows what makes a good caravan guard. He’s been paid well to work with Hsing (and the dragon’s mistress), but it isn’t enough for him to throw his life away.

He’ll seek to question and evaluate Hsing’s choices in companions until he’s satisfied.

## Chandra Stol (Follower of Mielikki) 

Location: [[Parnast Overview| Parnast]]

Chandra, a female half-elf, has only been in Parnast for half a year, but has lived just outside of the village for decades. Until very recently, she occasionally came into the village to leave small offerings to Mielikki (gifts of bread, berries, and small sacrifices), but avoided being seen due to the presence of the Cult of the Dragon. With their defeat, however, she has taken up permanent residence in the shrine. She is very quiet; she rarely speaks and is more likely to shrug in response to a question than give an answer other than “yes” and “no”.

## Raggnar Redtooth (Townsman)

Location: [[Parnast Overview| Parnast]]

During the cults occupation of Parnast, Raggnar accepted bribes from the cults leadership to conceal their activity and keep them abreast of any unusual activity within Parnast. Though he was outed for his conduct, he has managed t convince most of the townsfolk that he did what he did to ensure Parnast’s well-being.

Raggnar is a tall, heavyset man with enormous mutton chop sideburns who seems friendly.
