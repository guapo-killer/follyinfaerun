There, the elderly half-orc auctioneer **Leo Cloudfang** presented three major contracts, warning that dangers on the roads had increased and that only groups of five or more could take on official work.

### **The Three Available Contracts**

1. **Investigate a desert caravan route known as the Black Road.**
    
2. **Explore Old Marblehead Lighthouse, where strange lights have been seen.**
    
3. **Track down a hidden fortress tied to recent caravan attacks.**
    

Chose option 2

## Head out towards the Lighthouse

You are met by a goblin attack - you kill them and find they all have a branding on their arms. 

![[Screenshot 2025-11-20 150745.png]]

Proceed to investigate the Lighthouse - and find the remnants of the old lighthouse keeper's journal and other provisions. *(After Bilbafort gets bonked on the head)*

Learn from his journal that the Lighthouse had been operating as a location for smugglers. When the lighthouse keeper learnt that the smugglers were using it to intentionally lead ships towards the rocks, killing the crew & taking their cargo - he could not live with himself.

Continuing to the top of the tower, you found that the strange lights came from a Necromancing Acolyte that took up residence there, attempting to summon an ancient demon from the sea.
