# Arrival at Parnast: 

Your journey continues and soon you find yourself passing through the foothills of the Greypeaks. You camp for a final night before you reach Parnast, eager to finish the journey through the desert. You continue on with little delay for about half a day, and at noon you finally reach the town of Parnast. 

The guards of Parnast look you up and down distrustfully as you enter into the town, and unlike Daggerford, the streets of the town are quiet and the few people you see all appear distracted and un-noticing of you. 

## Rumour

- Gerald Thorles - horse breeder (**has another name to the party...**)

You speak to people at the inn and learn that Ragnor Redtooth is the closest thing the town has to a mayor, and that during their previous troubles with cultists that had taken up residence in the town, he had taken bribes to look the other way (supposedly to keep the town from suffering further)

## The Shrine

The shrine is kept by Chandra, and you help put up the new statue that had been destroyed by the cultists.

Learn that she is keeping 2 wounded dwarven men in her quarters - they have injuries from a supposed Zhent attack.

Decide to re-trace their steps and learn more about the Zhents nearby.

## Cult of the Dragon

Found an encampment where the Zhents were staying - kill all bar one, who expalins that they are posing as Zhents and are being paid by a Mage who lives in a cave nearby, that his followers call 'Chosen'

Storm the cave and learn they are attempting to perform some kind of ritual but unsure what they are attempting to summon or perform.

After a long battle, Victor secomes to his injuries. The party return his body back to Parnast hoping for Chandra to help resurrect him.

She explains she is not powerful enough to do such a ritual, in order to do so you must go to Karse where there may be a cleric powerful enough to help.
