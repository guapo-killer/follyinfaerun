## The Dawns Embrace

Head to the shrine and ask to speak with the Cleric -  [[Myrren Fernbrook]]

*“Entering the shine, the two trees continue on creating a thick roof above the area. Moss covers the floor of the shrine, and the beam you’d seen from outside bares down on a low alter in the centre of the room.”*

How to resurrect Victor:
I have been able to call on Pelor once before for a situation like this, but your friend is far more…complicated…

I see their very essence has long since been deeply wounded, and that scar on their soul creates a unique challenge. In order to save your friend, I must attempt to rescue both parts of their soul, independently of one-another. And failing to retrieve both will condemn them for eternity. 

To be certain of this - I require a specific artifact:

The Star Mounts hold many mysteries - not least being the supposed resting place of Karsus himself. Those mountains have a number of caverns and caves under them, and if I am to help your friend, I will need an artifact from there. 

Look to the Western side of the Mounts, and you should find a cavern across the river. It is said that eons ago a wandering artificer took residence there. The area lent itself to creating magically imbued artifacts, and the one I ask you to seek out is the Rod of Resurrection.  

With this, I intend to call upon Pelor and Mystra simultaneously and return both halves of your friend to their earthly body.
