## The Artificers Study

Lots of experimental equipment is found in the study, books vials, a lab & a library for the artificer.

You find the blueprints for a "clockwork soldier" and vials of alchemists fire.

Explore their study further - and find a number of failed attempts at creating what you interpret as the Rod of Ressurection

You find a room with two cabinets and a third where the rod would be housed.

Set off a trap and have to fight the two Clockwork Soldiers, there to protect the rod. You defeat them and remove the core of one of the constructs.

Find a book "Tale of the first floating city"

You continue on into the cavern - and finally come across Vera Windshadow: 

A mage that had been stuck in the cavern for days, wounded and hiding from the monsters that lurk within.

You earn her trust, and she tells you that she seeks the Rod as well - as she is part of the Order of the Divine Mantle, and that she requires it as she believes there to be a plot to assassinate the leader of her order.

You attempt to barter a deal for use of the Rod, in exchange for getting her out alive.

## Betrayal!!

Everett reveals himself as a traitor - attacking Vera, and the rest of the party.

Having finally been defeated, the party deeply weakened & Vera permanently losing her left eye, you cast speak with dead on Everett to understand his intentions.

1. He was sent to hunt Victor, and he will be hunted for the rest of his life
2. Lady Valeric set the bounty on his life
3. Anyone Lady Valeric commands will be coming to kill Victor
4. Lady Valeric is Victors sister
5. Victor is next in line to the title of house Ameranth.

Finally, it comes to your attention that Lady Valeric has been scrying for Everett - and may have heard your conversation...
