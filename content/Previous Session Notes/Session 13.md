In this session, you were able to dismantle the kidnapping and slavery operation that had been set up within the city of Arabel, posing as prospectors looking to engage in the 'lucrative' mining expedition posed to you by a strange elf at a bar.

- Having found the warehouse from which he was operating, you managed to sneak in and dispatch the guards that were posted there, without alerting the city guards, and set your ambush for their bosses return.
- After a battle with this arcane caster, you managed to take him hostage, and in doing so learnt that he had been hired by a Githyanki and a strangely dressed man that you concluded must have been from the order of the shattered peaks, that were luring miners from the area by using fake mounds of Mythril in order to kidnap them and send them to the Githyanki mine where you learnt they had been attempting to mine buoyancy stones, and construct - to your knowledge - the first Skyship within the land. 

- You dispatched the slaver, and Derek decided to let the gods decide their fate - flipping a coin with the insignia of their god Urogalan. 

- Batch and Bilbafort searched the city library for information on skyships, and of Urogalan - which you were told by the kind old gnome clerk that he would search their archives for the information you seek and will provide you with the knowledge you seek on your return.

- Victor had gone out into forests surrounding Arabel to check in on Billy who had agreed to complete the work on your Skyship for (what Batch believes to be - an exuberant rate), as well as drop off some supplies for him. 

 - So too, you were approached by an imposing figure - who introduced himself as Under-sword Rygarn Hales (Captain of the City Garrison).

- They extended their gratitude to your party for having returned the miners that had been captured by the Githyanki, providing you with a reward from the city - explaining that the city appreciates acts of good will and as such you should be rewarded for your actions. 

- The went on to explain that they have an additional task for yourselves if you were interested in assisting them. They explained that their boss -  Oversword Dutharr - had tasked himself and a local toymaker to make something special for his daughter, a box of many animals (a magical trinket, that when finished, would allow them to view magical replicas of nearly all animals found in the land).

	- He asked that you go speak with the toy maker Qisty within the city to discuss the un-mapped 'animal' that is most dear to little Rosie - an Ettercap....

![[ettercap.png]]

- You agreed to assist him, and headed to Qisty's toy shop - which was particularly busy given the upcoming festivities within the city. 

- She gave you 'Darwins Wand' - an artifact that allows you to magically document a creature (by being within a close distance to them), as well as the location for where she believed you could find an Ettercap to complete the box of many animals.

- You headed to your destination to find the Ettercap, entering its den - infested with spiders.

- Batch cleverly turned into a spider themselves - and in doing so, granted themself spider-sense as they tuned into the vibrations of the spider webs that filled the area. 

- Finally facing off with the Ettercap - you managed to document their visage, and dispatched them at last after a long fought battle. 

- You returned to the city - eagerly awaiting the completion of the work on your newly acquired Skyship & looking to retrieve the reward for your efforts to document the Ettercap.
