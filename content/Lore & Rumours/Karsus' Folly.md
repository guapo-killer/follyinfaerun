Long ago, a great magical kingdom lived high in the skies. Their cities lived atop the sheared peaks of mountains, held afloat by their immense magical power - focussed into the centre of their cities.

One faithful day, Karsus finished creating a spell that he had been developing for years. This spell would steal the power of a deity and transfer it to the archwizard that cast it, specifically designed to usurp the powers of his chosen deity. Karsus believed that with the power of a god at his disposal, he could destroy their enemies and unite his people.

You see, the Netherese people did not *truly* believe in Gods. Some say they even spited them, for they believed them to be nothing more than powerful wizards that had ascended to lofty highs, and cut the ladder out from below them to prevent anyone else from reaching them.

Therefore, when Karsus cast the spell he chose Mystryl, the goddess of magic, as his target, feeling that she was the most powerful deity and the most appropriate choice for his purposes. Thus Karsus briefly gained power over all magic in the universe. With his hands on the weave of magic itself, and the primal knowledge stored within it, he realised his terrible mistake. Mystryl was far more than a gatekeeper of magic in the land, she was the caretaker of the weave. Ever guiding it, nurturing it and taming it - and with her destruction, the weave rapidly deteriorated.

Karsus lacked the ability to maintain it himself, and it is said the magic of the world immediately surged and fluctuated, waterfalls began flowing upwards, snow rose from the ground and fires sapped warmth from around them.

With a dying gasp Mystryl sacrificed herself to reset the weave, and in her death, magic ceased to exists. The cities of Netheril plummeted from the skies, forever scaring the land with the memory of Karsus' folly.

The process is said to have killed Karsus, petrifying his very body in place - the consequence of his actions still etched into his expression.

In the aftermath, the Gods united to piece Mystryl back together, and she returned as Mystra, the ever tender of the weave.
