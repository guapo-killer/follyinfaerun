## Summary

The Tri-City Accord was struck some time in the past few years, forming an alliance between [[Neverwinter]], [[Waterdeep]], and [[Luskan]].

The accord is said to have been spearheaded by the newly elected Open Lord of Waterdeep -  [[Laeral Silverhand]], and outlines an allyship between the cities for both trade assistance and mutual protection. 

Part of this agreement included better cooperation between the cities for the patrolling of their respective realms. 

The people of the Sword Coast have become accustomed to the sight of three soldiers - one from each city, travelling the roads hoping to ward off  beasts and would-be bandits. This appears to have helped boost trade between the lesser towns in the surrounding areas, as far South as [[Daggerford Overview|Daggerford]].

Some townsfolk even speculate that these patrols occasionally stray into areas ruled by other kingdoms, but this is often chalked down to nothing more than 'honest mistakes'....
