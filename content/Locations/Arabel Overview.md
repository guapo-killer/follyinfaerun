# Summary

Arabel is a fortified merchant city in eastern Cormyr, known as the "Caravan City". It is situated at a major crossroads and is famous for jewellery and other goods, with a thriving trade economy and a history of independence. 

The city is currently ruled by Lord Myrmeen Lhal and features a large population of merchants, mercenaries, and adventurers. 

# Location and geography

![[arabel map.png]]

Arabel is located in the eastern part of the kingdom of Cormyr, at the intersection of several major trade routes, including Calantar's Way, the East Way, and the Moonsea Ride.

The city is a fortified stronghold, though it also has many trading posts and warehouses located outside its walls. 


# Economy and trade

Arabel is primarily a merchant city, known as the "Caravan City" or "Overland City".

Key exports: 

It is famous for its jewelry, particularly from the House of Thond, and is the main source of coal for Cormyr, which it gets from mines in the nearby Gnoll Pass. 

Other exports include horses, coal, dry red wine, and a black beer called Bitter Black.

Trading companies: Several major trading companies have a presence in the city, including the 
- Dragoneye Dealing 
- Six Coffers 
- Thousandheads
- Trueshield
- Iron Throne

Mercenaries: The city is a hub for mercenaries, with a constant pool of talent available for hire. 

# Politics and rulership

Ruler: The city is governed by Lord Myrmeen Lhal, a former adventurer appointed by the crown.

Merchant-friendly laws: Under Lhal's rule, merchants are given significant freedom as long as no harm is done and the city's independence is not threatened.

History: Arabel has a long history of rebellion and a degree of independence from the kingdom of Cormyr. 


# Culture and society

Free-reining: Unlike other Cormyrian cities, Arabel allows its inhabitants, including mercenaries, to carry their weapons openly.

Celebrations: During winter, Arabel residents celebrate High-harvestide and perform special prayers known as the "Gathering In".

# Community:  

Arabel is a frontier city that attracts newcomers, especially those without noble titles.
